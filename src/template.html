Stop 03 · Solar System

How physics found me

I study computer engineering, but the question that keeps pulling me is a physics one: how do we find a few interesting events hidden in billions of collisions? That is where computing and physics stop being separate subjects.

In Guayaquil there are very few places to study physics, so I found it on my own: through competitions, a school physics club, and later by teaching it as a teaching assistant for Mathematics and Physics at UEES.

My exchange at the Universidad de Oviedo turned that curiosity into practice. I now train neural networks on simulated CMS-style collision data, fit likelihoods like an experimental physicist, and write every notebook so someone else can learn from it.
