How physics found me

I study computer engineering in Guayaquil, Ecuador. Ever since I was a kid I have been passionate about technology and science, especially physics. Since I could not study physics at university, I went looking for it on my own: books, videos and every chance I got to learn more. I was part of my school's physics club, where I won two medals in national physics olympiads (bronze 2022 and silver 2023).

At university I became a Physics and Mathematics teaching assistant, and through my classes I have helped dozens of students while keeping alive my passion for teaching and for learning more. During my exchange at the Universidad de Oviedo I have been learning physics from a more academic side, and I discovered how my degree, data science and particle physics complement each other.
